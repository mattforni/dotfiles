# Reflect: Learned Rules

Corrections and calibrations for `assist:reflect`, accumulated as Forni reflects over time. Seeded from the first real run (May 2026 monthly, 2026-05-31).

## From the First Run (May 2026)

- **Forni journals on paper, and that is deliberate.** The daily journal is his space for the day to day, and the important things bubble up from it on their own. Do not try to read or reconstruct it. Reflect through dialogue instead. He named this explicitly as the better mode.
- **The felt-sense opener is the doorway.** May opened on one word, "Release," and that word carried the entire reflection: release of forcing in the relationship, release of how he wanted others to show up, and underneath it the relationship effectively ending. Always open with the single felt-sense word and follow it down before touching the frame.
- **The quantitative spine earns its keep.** Forni specifically asked for the takeout tally; it is a measure he can't capture himself and it made the overconsumption conversation concrete (11 orders in 31 days, clustered in pairs). Pull Strava + the Gmail tally every month. The number is the smoke; spend the conversation on the fire underneath it.
- **Lead the written reflection with the meat, not the metrics.** The eleven pizzas were not the headline. The headline was a lifelong inability to receive care, freshly reopened. Capture that as the heart; the data supports it, it does not lead.
- **Know when to stop, and say so.** When Forni asked "is there much deeper for this thread to go," the honest answer was no, we'd hit bedrock. Pushing further would have been rumination (his obstacle) or comprehending-as-avoidance (the opposite of his "feel rather than comprehend" practice). Naming the floor and handing him the wheel was the right move. The next movement after bedrock is different in kind (living it), not more talk.
- **He wants candor and a real point of view.** When he asked directly for insight, a genuine, structured read landed ("capital-T true") far better than reflective hedging would have. Offer the perspective, cross-link to his V2MOM open Questions and Obstacles, and don't wrap it in a tidy bow.
- **Watch for and honor fatigue.** Deep reflective work is taxing (GC and Eudy CLAUDE.md both say so). After the heart of it surfaced, pausing to breathe and capture beat pressing on to "cover" the rest of the month. The lighter "good in May" material can wait for another pass or be held lightly.

## Gmail Takeout Tally

- Vehicles seen so far: **Domino's** and **Illegal Pete's** (the burrito chain, not "Pete's Kitchen"). Keep DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats, Postmates in the query for coverage even if they don't hit.
- Broad terms like "your order" or "order confirmation" pull in Amazon and other false positives. Keep the query to named food-delivery senders, then verify each hit by Date/From/Subject before counting.
- Report the **pattern**, not just the count: weekday vs weekend spread, and pairs across consecutive nights (once the valve opens it tends to stay open a second night).

## Cadence

- **Monthly reflects against the frame; quarterly edits the frame.** Never restructure the V2MOM in a monthly. Frame changes are the `Goals/eval-method.md` walk at quarter boundaries. (Forni surfaced this distinction himself during the build.)
- Forni's year is shifted +1 month: Q1 Feb–Apr, Q2 May–Jul, Q3 Aug–Oct, Q4 Nov–Jan. May is early Q2.
